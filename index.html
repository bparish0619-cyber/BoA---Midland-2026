Use the strict bag rule

Bring a clear bag or a small bag no larger than 6×9×5 inches. BOA separately lists clear bags up to 12×6×12, so the smaller venue limit is the safest choice.

Still photos only

Cameras are allowed from general-admission seats. No field-level photography, tripods, monopods, video, or audio recording.

Food stays outside

No outside food or drinks in the stadium. The band picnic area is at Coterra Pavilion between the stadium and ballpark. Concessions close during the dinner break.

Separate spectator parking

Use the east spectator lot from the Loop 250 frontage road or the southwest lot near Momentum Bank Ballpark from Tradewinds Boulevard. Do not enter through the bus/truck gate.

Lot routing comes from the 2022 official site plan. A 2026 spectator parking charge was not published.

Quick answers

Useful details for a long competition day.

When should spectators be seated for Wylie?

Aim to be through the gate and seated by 12:15 PM. That leaves 30 minutes before Wylie’s 12:45 PM performance for parking, screening, and finding seats. This buffer is a practical recommendation, not an official event time.

Can we stay in the stadium between prelims and finals?

No. The stadium is cleared after preliminary awards and reopened for finals at 6:30 PM. A prelims-only ticket does not include the finals session.

What should parents carry?

Digital tickets, a payment card or mobile wallet, sunscreen, a hat, a light evening layer, a phone battery pack, and hearing protection for anyone sensitive to loud sound. Keep bags within the stricter 6×9×5-inch venue limit or use a compliant clear bag.

Is parking free?

The 2022 official event guide listed free spectator parking, but the 2026 page does not confirm a price. Treat parking cost as unverified and have a card ready.
